02 · From insight to action

A focused 30-day plan

Start with the 42 loss orders, then run a controlled discount test and add a weekly exception review.

01

Classify the 42 loss orders

Review product, discount, region, channel, quantity, and unit cost. Tag each loss with a reason such as promotion, price override, cost mismatch, or data issue.

Weeks 1–2 · Pricing + Finance
02

Test Electronics offer guardrails

Compare a lower discount tier or approval step for deeper promotions with a matched baseline. Track profit per order and conversion together.

Weeks 2–4 · Commercial + Pricing
03

Monitor order-level exceptions

Review negative-profit orders and discount exceptions weekly, with product, region, and channel drilldowns. Track loss count and total negative profit.

Weekly · BI + Finance
04

Protect total business performance

Review category sales, profit, margin, and discount quartiles together. Set discount thresholds only after confirming cost and promotion economics.

Monthly · Category leads
How to interpret the analysis

These are descriptive summaries from the existing retail sales data. Product groups remain profitable in aggregate even when some individual transactions lose money. Discount guardrails are a test to validate, not a guaranteed outcome.
